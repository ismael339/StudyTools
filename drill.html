Study Coach Drills — How It Works and Why It Matters

Generic quizzes don't help you improve. Our Study Coach Drills build quizzes from the topics you keep getting wrong — not from a generic question bank. Every drill targets your weak areas so you spend your study time where it matters most.

What Is a Study Drill?

A study drill is a targeted quiz built from your own material. Instead of answering random questions, you drill on the specific concepts, formulas, and facts you struggle with. This is targeted practice, and it's one of the most effective ways to raise your grades.

How to Use Study Coach Drills Step by Step

  1. Paste your notes: Copy your study material into the text box.
  2. Choose a mode: Select quiz mode for practice questions, or coach mode for explanations.
  3. Generate your drill: The AI builds questions based on your material and your weak topics.
  4. Review your results: See which topics you need to work on and track your progress over time.

Why Targeted Drills Beat Generic Quizzes

Most quiz apps show you the same questions to everyone. Our drills adapt to you — if you keep getting calculus derivatives wrong, you'll see more derivative questions until you master them. This personalized approach saves time and improves retention.

Pro Tip: Combine with Active Recall

Use your drills alongside active recall — try to answer before looking at the explanation, then review what you got wrong. This combination is proven to strengthen long-term memory.
