How this study planner works

Enter your exam date, your subjects and the hours you can honestly give each day. The planner spreads revision across the days that are left, gives the topics you keep failing the longer blocks, and rebuilds the day-by-day timetable every time something changes: a missed day, a new subject or a topic that finally clicked.

How far ahead should I plan? Keep the days until your exam exact and plan the rest week by week. The planner does the same maths you would do on paper, only it redoes it when your situation changes.

What if I miss a day? Nothing breaks. Open the planner again and the remaining topics move into the days that are left, hardest topics first.

Can I revise several subjects at once? Yes. Add every subject you are revising and the timetable shares the available hours between them instead of letting one subject eat the whole week.
