About

A study toolkit that does one thing well.

StudyTools is an independent project, not a startup with a growth team behind it.

It started from a simple frustration: the tools that help you study are either full of ads, or they ask for a subscription before you have seen anything useful. So StudyTools is built the other way round. Every tool is free to try, the free plan is genuinely usable, and Pro exists for the students who hit the daily limit during exam season.

What we will not do

No ads on the study pages

An advert in the middle of an explanation is an advert in the middle of your revision.

No selling your data

What you type stays between you and the tool that answers it. Read the privacy notice.

No fake urgency

Countdown timers and fake discounts are not a business model, they are a trick.

No lock-in

Cancel Pro from PayPal in two clicks. Your saved work stays in your account either way.

What Pro pays for

Pro costs 3.99 EUR a month or 29.99 EUR a year. That money covers the AI usage itself, the servers and the email that tells you when a new tool ships. It is a small operation on purpose: no sales team, no call centre, no account managers. If Pro stops covering its costs we will say so on this page before changing anything.

Accuracy over speed

The AI features show their reasoning so you can check them, and the terms say plainly that they can be wrong. The study guides are written the long way round: no filler, no lists of 40 items, just what actually moves a grade.

What is in the toolkit

All of it free to start: an AI tutor, an exam solver, a GPA calculator, a flashcard maker, a study planner and a Pomodoro timer, plus study guides that explain the methods behind them.

Talk to us

Requests for new tools, corrections and complaints all go to the same inbox: support@studytools.pro. We answer within one working day, usually the same day. More on the contact page.
