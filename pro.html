Questions before you pay

The things students actually ask us.

What exactly do I get with Pro?

Your daily AI allowance goes from 15 to 2000 requests, answers stop being cut short, and your chats, study sets, solved questions, flashcards and citations are stored in your account. Sign in on a library computer or your phone and everything is there.

Why is it so cheap?

Because we do not run a sales team or charge per seat. There is one product, one price and one thing to pay for. Pro costs less than one hour of tutoring, and most students tell us it pays for itself the first time it saves an hour.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your PayPal account: Payments, then Manage automatic payments, then cancel StudyTools. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again. There is no cancellation email to send and no retention call.

What if I only need it for a few weeks?

Then take Pro Monthly for the exam season and cancel afterwards. If the school year is what you are paying for, the yearly plan costs about 2.50 EUR a month, which is less than a coffee a week.

Is my card safe?

We never see your card. Payment happens on PayPal's servers, and we only receive a subscription reference. Pro is linked to your StudyTools account, not to your card details.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The free plan needs no payment details at all, and the calculators, timer, flashcards and citation generator work without an account.

Can my school block it?

Your school decides what AI use is allowed. StudyTools shows the reasoning so you can check each step against your course material, and the study guides explain how to use it without handing in work that is not yours.
